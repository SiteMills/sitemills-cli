# SiteMills Acceptable Use Policy (AUP)

This document establishes the acceptable use standards for all code, content, applications, and network activity hosted on or generated through SiteMills.

## 1. Strictly Prohibited Activities & Content

Users may not generate, deploy, host, transmit, or facilitate any of the following prohibited categories:

### A. Mature & Sexually Explicit Content
- Pornography, sexually explicit text, images, animations, audio, or video.
- Adult games, erotic roleplay platforms, adult dating apps, or escort services.
- Non-consensual sexual content, synthetic intimate imagery, or deepfakes.
- **Zero Tolerance for CSAM/CSAE**: Any depiction, generation, or transmission of Child Sexual Abuse Material or Exploitation triggers immediate account termination, project destruction, and mandatory reporting to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and federal law enforcement.

### B. Scams, Fraud & Consumer Deception
- Phishing, credential harvesting, spoofed authentication flows, or deceptive branding.
- Financial scams, Ponzi schemes, multi-level marketing (MLM), fake cryptocurrency giveaways, or fraudulent investment platforms.
- Deceptive e-commerce storefronts, non-delivery scams, or dark patterns designed to mislead consumers into recurring charges.

### C. Platform Abuse & Infrastructure Integrity
- Attempting sandbox escapes, tampering with runtime container boundaries, or accessing unauthorized host memory or namespaces.
- Generating or distributing malware, trojans, ransomware, spyware, keyloggers, botnets, or command-and-control servers.
- Denial of Service (DoS/DDoS) attacks, packet flooding, unauthorized port scanning, or vulnerability probing.
- Cryptocurrency mining or routing unauthorized third-party LLM traffic through platform quotas.
- Registering multiple accounts to circumvent usage quotas, billing tiers, or enforcement suspensions.

### D. Illegal & Illicit Activity
- Controlled substances, illegal narcotics, unlicensed firearms or weapons, human trafficking, stolen property, or unlicensed gambling.
- Any activity violating municipal, state, federal, or international laws.

### E. Hate Speech & Violence
- Discrimination, harassment, cyberbullying, doxxing, or hate speech targeting protected groups.
- Incitement, promotion, or instruction of violence, terrorism, self-harm, or suicide.

## 2. Website Owner Accountability & Content Moderation

Project owners are strictly and directly responsible for all code, content, transactions, and end-user interactions on their hosted applications:

- **User-Generated Content**: If an application allows external end users to post comments, upload files, create marketplace listings, or chat, the project owner **must actively prevent, monitor, and moderate illicit activity**.
- **Safe Harbor Requirements**: To remain in good standing when hosting user-generated content, owners must:
  1. Publish clear rules prohibiting illegal and abusive acts.
  2. Implement proactive technical filters and moderation mechanisms.
  3. Swiftly investigate abuse reports, remove illicit content, and ban offending end users.
  4. Promptly cooperate with valid law enforcement inquiries.

Failing to maintain active moderation or passively tolerating illicit activity constitutes a material breach and results in immediate project suspension.
